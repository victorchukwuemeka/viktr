I'm a protocol engineer. I work on Solana internals in Rust, and I've had
changes merged into SPL Token-2022, SPL Token, Pinocchio and Agave.

Most of my work is a light client called [sg32](https://github.com/victorchukwuemeka/sg32)
— it receives live shreds over the network, reconstructs blocks with Reed-Solomon
erasure coding, and generates Merkle proofs of transaction inclusion. No full node,
no RPC provider to trust.

Before that I built production cross-chain bridge infrastructure at Odinala, and a
B.Sc. in Biotechnology.

I write about the parts that break: wire formats, byte-level debugging, and the
things Solana's docs don't tell you.
