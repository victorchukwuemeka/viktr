## What I do

I build cross-chain infrastructure — bridges, relayers, and the Rust and Solidity
underneath them. Currently a software developer working on bridging SOL to EVM
networks, and building developer tooling on the side.

## What I write about

Cross-chain protocol design, Rust, Solidity, distributed systems, and the
engineering around deploying and monitoring services that other people depend on.

I'm not going to tell you I'm an expert in any of it. I'm going to write down
what I build, how it broke, and what I learned. If that's useful to you, good.
